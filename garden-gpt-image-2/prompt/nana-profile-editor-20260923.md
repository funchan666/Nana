# Profile editor artwork

Mode: built-in image tool, Garden Host-Native. Assets saved in `NanaHarbor/Assets.xcassets/NanaProfileEditorHero.imageset` and `NanaProfileEditorButton.imageset`.

## hero

Create original raster UI artwork for a native iOS social app named Nana. Asset only, NOT a screenshot. Landscape 3:2 edge-to-edge dark aubergine and midnight-violet personal identity card backdrop. Left 65 percent is almost flat very dark violet with extremely subtle soft fine grain, intentionally empty for a user's real avatar and readable white text. At the far upper right, a beautifully crafted small pearlescent lilac four-point sculptural star floats above two thin translucent iridescent curved ribbons, tiny warm pink reflected light; confined to the rightmost quarter. A gentle satin lilac light wash at the top right fading into the dark field. Restrained premium editorial style, sophisticated soft materials, no neon border, no glowing outline, no glass panel, no UI controls, no text, no typography, no logos, no people. Rounded corners will be applied by the app; render a full rectangular image without outside margins. Polished designed background for a compact profile editing hero.

## button

Create a single original blank primary action button bitmap for a native iOS social app. Center one very wide horizontal rounded rectangle, width-to-height about 6:1, gently rounded corners about 20 percent of height, NOT an oval or bulging pill. Button face saturated royal violet with a subtle lilac satin reflection toward the upper edge, extremely restrained depth and soft shadow. Crisp clean modern premium UI, no chrome rim, no outline border, no extravagant glow, no objects, no text, no letters, no icons. Genuine transparent background around the button, small 4 percent margin. Landscape composition. This blank asset will carry a white live text label supplied by the app.
